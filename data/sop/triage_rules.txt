SAMPLE TRIAGE RULES (Part D should refine these)

GENERAL
SOP-GEN-1  Critical: immediate threat to life, people trapped or missing, or a mass casualty risk.
SOP-GEN-2  High: serious damage or injuries, but people are not in immediate life danger.
SOP-GEN-3  Medium: property damage or disruption, no injuries reported.
SOP-GEN-4  Low: minor issue, no injuries, no spreading risk.
SOP-GEN-5  Unverified single reports with no photo or document: do not go above High unless people are explicitly trapped.

FLOOD
SOP-FLOOD-1  Water above knee height with people stranded: Critical. Dispatch rescue boat and alert nearest hospital.
SOP-FLOOD-2  Water on roads, vehicles stuck, no people stranded: Medium. Issue traffic advisory.
SOP-FLOOD-3  Water above 1 metre or fast flowing: at least High.

FIRE
SOP-FIRE-1  Fire in an occupied building: Critical. Dispatch fire brigade and ambulance.
SOP-FIRE-2  Fire in an unoccupied structure or open area: High. Dispatch fire brigade.

BUILDING COLLAPSE
SOP-COLLAPSE-1  Any collapse with people possibly trapped: Critical. Dispatch search and rescue and medical teams.
SOP-COLLAPSE-2  Cracks or partial damage, building evacuated: Medium. Request structural inspection.

MEDICAL
SOP-MED-1  Unconscious, not breathing, or severe bleeding: Critical. Dispatch ambulance.
SOP-MED-2  Injury, stable patient: High. Dispatch ambulance.

EVIDENCE
SOP-EVID-1  If text and photo disagree, flag the conflict and send to human review.
